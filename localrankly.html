Google Maps SEO

How to Rank #1 on Google Maps in Dhaka, Bangladesh (2025 Guide)

👤 LocalRankly Team📅 April 2025⏱ 12 min read
Quick Summary: To rank #1 on Google Maps in Dhaka, you need to: (1) fully optimize your Google Business Profile, (2) build consistent NAP citations, (3) generate genuine reviews, and (4) create locally-relevant content. Read on for the full step-by-step breakdown.

1. Optimize Your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single most important factor for Google Maps ranking. A complete and accurate profile tells Google exactly what your business does, where it operates, and who it serves.

Make sure every field is filled in: business name, address, phone number, website, hours, category (primary and secondary), photos, and description. Add at least 10 high-quality photos.

2. Build Local Citations

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) on other websites. Consistent citations across directories like Yellow Pages Bangladesh, Bangladesh Business Directory, and industry-specific sites significantly improve your local authority.

3. Get More Google Reviews

Reviews are a major Google Maps ranking factor. Ask every satisfied customer to leave a review. Respond to all reviews — positive and negative — professionally and promptly. Aim for a 4.5+ star rating with 50+ reviews.

💡 Pro Tip: The fastest way to get Google reviews is to text your happy customers a direct link to your Google review page right after their visit or purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to rank #1 on Google Maps in Dhaka?+
On average, 2-4 months for low-competition niches, 4-8 months for competitive niches like restaurants, dentists, and lawyers in central Dhaka.
Is Google Business Profile free?+
Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is completely free. You only need a Google account and a verified business address.
